# Sogon Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01  
Operator: un-veil ("we")  
Contact: support@un-veil.co.kr

> **Notice of upcoming change (posted 2026-11-12)**  
> We plan to change this policy when we release a version of the app (0.5.0 or later) that adds a "text in photos" search feature. The change takes effect **on the day that version is released, which will be 2026-11-19 or later**, and the revised policy will be posted on this page before it takes effect.
>
> - The feature is planned to be **off by default**; only if you turn it on in Settings will the app read the text in your photos **on your device** and store it in the app's private storage.
> - The text it reads is not sent to our servers, and turning the feature off is planned to delete all of it.
> - How the items already covered by this policy are handled does not change.

We provide the Android app "Sogon" (the "app"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where, and what rights you have.

## 1. At a glance

- The app has no sign-up or login and **we do not operate any server for it.**
- Your photos, videos, search queries, the analysis results of your photos, and the capture locations recorded in your photos are **processed and stored only on your device and are never sent to us.**

## 2. What the app handles and how

| Information | Purpose | Where it is processed and stored | Sent to our servers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photos and videos (files and details your device provides, such as capture time, file name, album name) | Show the library, search, analysis | On your device. The app only reads them, and deletes only when you choose to, through the system | No |
| Analysis results (numeric search values, "sea/food"-type content suggestions, analysis progress) | Search by sentence or content, similar photos | App-private storage on your device | No |
| Capture location stored in your photos (only if you allow the "photo location" permission) | Search by place name such as "Incheon" | Converted on the device into a neighborhood-level ID using an offline place-name dictionary bundled in the app; **the coordinates are discarded** (not stored, logged or sent). The app does not read your device's current location | No |
| Search queries and recent searches | Search, show recent searches | On your device; you can clear them in Settings | No |
| Favorites and display settings | App features | On your device | No |
| Notifications | Show analysis progress (if you allow notifications) | On your device | No |

App data is excluded from Android automatic backup (`allowBackup=false`).

## 3. When the app uses the internet

- The app itself does not use the internet to send your photos, queries or analysis results.
- In versions that provide features needing the search model (about 630 MB), the model is **downloaded separately by Google Play** after install and delivered to the app (over Wi-Fi by default). This is a Google Play service and we do not receive your information. After the model is downloaded, you can search without an internet connection.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Information the app stores on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. "Clear history" in Settings removes only your search history.
- We do not keep personal information on any server, so we have no separate retention or deletion procedure.
- Photos you ask the app to delete are handled under Android's system trash rules.

## 5. Sharing, processors and international transfers

- We do not share your personal information with third parties or hand it to processors.

## 6. Your rights

- Information stored only on your device can be removed by you in the app (clear history), by clearing app data, or by uninstalling.
- You can change or withdraw photo and location permissions at any time in your device settings. Withdrawing location permission does not remove place identifiers already stored; clearing app data does.

- To request access, correction, deletion or restriction, email support@un-veil.co.kr. We will answer within the period required by law, and tell you if we hold no information about you.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 14 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

## 8. Security

- Information is processed and stored on your device only by design, and we operate no server.
- Requested permissions are limited to what is needed: reading photos/videos, reading photo locations, notifications, and the analysis task.
- App data is excluded from cloud backup.

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not use cookies.
