# Hushspark Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20  
Operator: un-veil ("we")  
Contact: support@un-veil.co.kr

We provide the Android rhythm action game "Hushspark" (package name `com.hushspark.game`, the "app"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it keeps it.

## 1. At a glance

- We do **not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information through the app.
- The app has no sign-up or login, no ads, no payments, and no analytics or tracking tools.
- Game records and settings are stored **only on your device** and are never sent to us.
- We do not operate any server.

## 2. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers. The app has no sign-up or login.

## 3. Information stored only on your device

The app stores your game records (best progress per level, clear status, number of attempts) and settings (volume, timing calibration, effects intensity) only on your device. This information is never sent off your device and is not shared with anyone, including us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## 4. Internet and third parties

The app does not send any information over the internet. It does not use advertising, payments, or analytics tools, and it does not share information with third parties. When you tap "Privacy Policy" in the app's settings, your device's web browser opens this page. That internet access is made by the browser, not by the app.

## 5. Permissions

The app does not request device permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage.

## 6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If a change to the app changes how information is handled, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. The change history is kept at the bottom of this page.

## 8. Contact

Privacy questions: support@un-veil.co.kr
