# Minus March Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10  
Operator: un-veil ("we")  
Contact: support@un-veil.co.kr

We provide the Android subtraction number puzzle game "Minus March" (package name `com.tinpawn.minusmarch`, the "app") for children aged 5 to 8. This policy explains what information the app handles when a child uses it, where it keeps it, who it may reach, and what rights **parents and guardians** have.

## 1. At a glance

### For kids (easy version)

- This game never asks about you. It doesn't know your name, your picture, or where you live.
- Your stars and stickers stay inside this device. We don't get them.
- There are no ads.
- If you have a question, ask a parent or a grown-up you live with.

### Summary for parents

- We do **not collect, store, or transmit** any personal information from children or parents through the app.
- The app has no ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking tools, no crash-reporting tools, no accounts or sign-in, and no chat or sharing features.
- Game progress is stored only on the device and is never sent to us.
- We operate no server.
- The one-time purchase is processed by Google Play; we never receive payment details.
- All external links in the app (this policy, the store page, the support email) are only on the parents' screens behind an adult check (parental gate).

## 2. What the app handles and how

### 2.1 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect the following through the app.

- No personal information of any kind: no name, email, phone number, date of birth, photos, voice recordings, location, or contacts.
- We do not show ads and do not use the advertising ID.
- We do not use analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration, or tracking tools.
- There is no account creation, sign-in, chat, or photo sharing, and no feature for exchanging information with other people.
- We operate no server, and the app does not send game records over the internet.

### 2.2 Information stored only on the device

So that a child can continue playing, the following is stored **only in the app's private storage on the device**. It is never sent to us, and we cannot see it.

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| Game progress | Chosen track, current level, stars and achievements per level, whether the tutorial was completed, and whether certain one-time screens were already shown |
| Stickers and decorations | Collected stickers and chosen decorations |
| Settings | Voice, music, sound effects, vibration, reduced shaking, and fast march on/off |
| Purchase status | Whether "Unlock all levels" was purchased and when Google Play last confirmed it (so that purchased levels stay open without an internet connection) |

- This information is deleted when the app is uninstalled or when its data is cleared in the device settings.
- If Android backup (Google account backup) is turned on for the device, Android may include these game records in the parent's Google account backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's terms and privacy policy, and we cannot access it.
- The app does not store how many times a child failed a level or how many hints were shown.

### 2.3 What the app does not use

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo/file storage, call log, SMS, or accessibility services. It does not receive your name, email, or account information.

## 3. Permissions the app requests

| Permission | What it is used for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Vibration | A short vibration during play. It can be turned off in the app settings | A normal permission granted automatically at install; no prompt |
| Google Play billing (`com.android.vending.BILLING`) | Buying and checking the one-time item | Payments are processed by Google Play (section 5) |
| Network state | Used by the Google Play billing component to check connection status | A normal permission granted automatically at install |

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or advertising ID permissions, and shows no permission prompts.

## 4. Ads and analytics

The app has no ads and uses no ad tools (such as AdMob) and no advertising ID. It also contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. So no information is sent from the app to us or to any advertising or analytics company for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## 5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

- The first 10 levels of each track are free. "Unlock all levels" is a one-time purchase, and the purchase screen opens only after an adult check (parental gate). Children's screens show no price and no purchase button.
- Payments are processed by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google Play handles your payment method and Google account information; the app and we cannot see them.
- Through the Google Play billing component (provided by Google LLC), the app only asks whether the item has been purchased and stores the result on the device (section 2.2). It does not send purchase records to any server of ours.
- If a purchase needs a parent's approval (for example in a family group), the app keeps it pending until the approval is confirmed, and only then opens the levels.
- Google Play provides us, as the seller, with order records through Google Play Console (such as order number, item, price and tax, order date and status), and we can look up an order by the buyer's Google account email. We use this information **only to handle refunds and support requests**, and do not use it for any other purpose or share it with third parties.

## 6. Sharing, processors, and transfers abroad

- Because we do not collect personal information through the app, there is nothing to share, sell, or hand to a processor. We do not share information from parent support emails with third parties except where required by law.
- Google Play billing (section 5) and Android backup (section 2.2) are handled by Google, which may process information outside Korea (for example in the United States).
- Information the Google Play store itself collects when you install and use the app from Google Play (such as install statistics, or crash statistics from devices whose users agreed to share them) is governed by Google Play's policies.

## 7. Retention and deletion

- Game records on the device are deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.
- We hold no app information on any server, so we have nothing of that kind to keep or delete.
- We use the email address and message in a parent's support email only to reply and to process refunds, keep them for one year after the request is resolved, and then delete them. Please do not include a child's personal information (such as name or photo) in your message.
- Google Play order records are kept under Google Play's policies.

## 8. Your rights and how to use them

- The only information we hold is parents' support emails and Google Play order records. To access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of it, contact support@un-veil.co.kr. We will confirm and reply within the period required by law, and if we hold nothing about you, we will say so.
- Game records on the device are deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the device settings.
- "Contact us" in the app (behind the adult check) opens the parent's own email app. The app does not attach device information or game records to the email.
- If a child made a purchase by mistake or without permission, please contact us. After confirming, we will process a refund through Google Play. Game progress, stars, and stickers are kept after a refund.

## 9. Children's personal information

The app is for children aged 5 to 8, and we do not receive personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we accidentally receive a child's personal information (for example, in a support email), we delete anything not needed to reply as soon as we notice it.

## 10. Security measures

- Game records are kept only in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
- The app's code has no feature that sends user information to our servers or to third parties, and it contains no ad or analytics tools.
- External links appear only on parents' screens behind the adult check (parental gate); children's screens have no links to a web browser or other apps.

## 11. Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not use cookies.
